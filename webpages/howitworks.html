The Full Process

  1. Sign up — Create your account and tell us about your dietary preferences and goals.
  2. Browse the menu — Each week, our chefs design a new selection of 20+ meals across all cuisines.
  3. Place your order — Select your meals by Wednesday at midnight for the following week's delivery.
  4. We cook fresh — Your meals are prepared on delivery day for maximum freshness and flavor.
  5. Doorstep delivery — Meals arrive in insulated boxes with ice packs to keep everything at the right temperature.
  6. Heat and enjoy — Most meals reheat in 3-4 minutes. Some are ready to eat right away!
